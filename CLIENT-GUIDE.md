# Your Website Dashboard: Quick Guide

Your dashboard is at **`https://your-website/admin`**. Sign in with your **access code**.
Sessions last 8 hours. After 5 wrong tries, the sign-in locks for 15 minutes.

Changes go live as soon as you click **Save**. There's no separate publish step.

## The pages

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | See product counts and unread messages, plus a checklist of what's left to set up. |
| **Products** | Add, edit, hide, or delete products. Each one has a name, brand, category, description, photo, price text (for example "$24.99" or "From $15"), stock status, and spec lines. **Featured** products show on the homepage. Hidden products stay saved but don't appear on the site. |
| **Categories** | Create the groups your products sit in (Disposables, E-liquids, Coils…), give each a photo, and use the arrows to change the order. Renaming a category updates its products automatically. |
| **Homepage** | The big headline and photo at the top, the promo banner, the About text and photo, and switches to show or hide homepage sections. |
| **Branding** | Shop name, tagline, logo, browser-tab icon (favicon), and social media links. |
| **Appearance** | Colors, fonts, button shape, header and footer style, and an optional background image. |
| **Store info** | Address, map, phone, email, weekly hours, holiday hours, and store policies. |
| **FAQ** | Questions and answers for the FAQ page. Use the arrows to change the order, or hide one without deleting it. |
| **Messages** | Messages from the website's contact form. Mark them read or unread, reply, or delete. If email replies are turned on, your reply is emailed to the customer. Otherwise it's saved here so you can call or email them yourself. |
| **Team access** | The owner can give staff their own access codes (up to 6 in total), reset a staff code, or remove someone. Removing a person signs them out immediately. Staff can edit the website but can't manage the team. |
| **SEO & legal** | The page title and description that Google shows, the age-check wording and minimum age, and your Privacy Policy, Terms, and compliance text. |
| **Security** | Change your own access code, and sign out. Changing your code signs out your other devices. |

## Tips

- **Photos:** JPG, PNG, or WebP. Big photos are shrunk automatically before upload.
  Wide photos work best for the homepage and About sections.
- **Placeholder text:** anything you leave blank shows sample text. Fill in every field
  on **Store info** and **Branding** before you share the site.
- **Legal text:** the sample Privacy, Terms, and age-check wording is a starting point,
  not legal advice. Check your state and local rules.
- **Lost your code?** Team members: ask the owner for a new one. Owner: click
  **Lost your access code?** on the sign-in screen and enter the **setup key** you were
  given when the site was set up. If you don't have it, contact whoever set up your website.
